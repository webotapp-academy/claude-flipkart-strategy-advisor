# Flipkart Strategy Advisor Skill

A Claude skill that applies the 10 proven growth lessons from Flipkart's journey to any Indian small or medium business — delivering a personalised, actionable strategy guide.

---

## What This Skill Does

1. Collects business details via a **3-step interactive form** (dropdowns, toggles, text fields)
2. Analyses the business against all **10 Flipkart growth principles**
3. Delivers a **personalised strategy guide** with specific actions for that business
4. Includes a **90-Day Roadmap** and **Investment Table**
5. Captures leads via webhook to `hook.webotapp.com`

---

## Flipkart's 10 Lessons Applied

| Step | Flipkart Lesson | Applied To |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Solve the trust problem first (CoD) | Trust mechanisms for your business |
| 2 | Start narrow, expand deliberately | Hero product / service focus |
| 3 | Scarcity and exclusivity sell (flash sales) | Offers and urgency tactics |
| 4 | Growth without profit is borrowed time | Unit economics and pricing |
| 5 | Build your brand before you need it | Content and brand strategy |
| 6 | Build a customer database (not just followers) | WhatsApp, email, lead capture |
| 7 | Kill what isn't working fast | 80/20 marketing audit |
| 8 | Know your legal and financial structure | Compliance and financial basics |
| 9 | Focus on the 20% that drives 80% revenue | Pareto audit |
| 10 | Protect your equity and vision | Systems, SOPs, long-term control |

---

## Trigger Phrases

This skill activates when the user says things like:

- "How do I grow my business?"
- "Give me a marketing strategy"
- "Apply Flipkart strategy to my business"
- "Business improvement plan"
- "How to get more customers"
- "Startup lessons for my business"
- "Brand building strategy India"

---

## Form Fields Collected

### Stage 1 — Business Basics
- Owner name, business name
- Business type (17 categories)
- City, years in operation, team size
- Website URL

### Stage 2 — Current Growth Status
- Monthly revenue range
- Where customers come from (multi-select)
- Advertising status
- Social media presence
- Customer database status
- Biggest current challenge

### Stage 3 — Goals & Vision
- Primary 12-month goal
- Target customer description
- Competitors
- Monthly marketing budget
- Which Flipkart strategy interests them most
- Contact details (optional)

## Installation

1. Download or clone this repository
2. Upload `SKILL.md` to your Claude skills folder (GitHub or local `/mnt/skills/user/`)
3. The skill auto-triggers based on the description keywords
